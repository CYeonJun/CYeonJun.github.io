Should an LLM reflect the world as it is, or correct toward how it should be? This user study compares how people perceive two competing standards for LLM bias:

- **Reality-reflecting:** outputs mirror real-world distributions and patterns
- **Norm-correcting:** outputs are adjusted toward normative, debiased ideals
