**WarFrame** is a counterfactual benchmark that measures how large language models frame geopolitical narratives — and whether that framing shifts depending on which actors are involved.

- **Data:** ~32K conflict events from ACLED
- **Design:** 300 counterfactual templates that swap actors while holding the event fixed
- **Evaluation:** 6 LLMs across 3 tasks
